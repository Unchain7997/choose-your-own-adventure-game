# Choose Your Own Adventure

A console adventure in which a player creates a character, picks a path through the mountains, and meets what waits at the end of it.

## Encounters

**Dragon Encounter**:
The scene at the end of the North path where the player meets the dragon and chooses to attack or retreat.
_Avoid_: Boss fight, dragon battle

**Merchant**:
Old Brannoc, the travelling seller the player meets on the South path. His name is the same in every language.
_Avoid_: Trader, shopkeeper, vendor, shop

**Merchant Encounter**:
The one-time scene on the South path where the player meets the Merchant, may buy Items with Gold, and then returns to the path choice.
_Avoid_: Shop, store, shopping

## Character

**Gold**:
The player's money, rolled when the character is created and spent only with the Merchant.
_Avoid_: Coins, money, currency

## Items

**Item**:
Something the Merchant sells that permanently raises one of the player's stats the moment it is bought. An Item is never carried or used later.
_Avoid_: Ware, product, goods, inventory item

**Healing Potion**:
An Item that raises the player's HealthPoints.
_Avoid_: Health potion, potion of healing

**Strength Tonic**:
An Item that raises the player's Strength.
_Avoid_: Strength potion

**Agility Elixir**:
An Item that raises the player's Agility.
_Avoid_: Agility potion, speed potion
